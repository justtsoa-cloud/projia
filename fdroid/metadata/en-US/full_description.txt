Tontolo is a free, local-first AI assistant. It picks the best free model for each request and
switches by itself when a quota runs out. You use your own free keys; there is no middleman server.

- Chat, learn mode with animated explainer videos, voice conversation, images, PDF and documents
- In three languages: French, English and Malagasy
- Your data stays on your device; minimal optional statistics, nothing hidden
- Open source (Apache-2.0)
